# Scientific Methodology and Workflow Documentation

This document describes the scientific principles, computational tools, parameter justifications, and quality control criteria implemented in the **Staphylococcus Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS) Assembly and Annotation Pipeline**.

---

## 1. Biological and Genomic Background

*Staphylococcus aureus* and related staphylococci are clinically significant Gram-positive pathogens responsible for both healthcare-associated and community-acquired infections. High-throughput Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS) is essential for:
- Detecting antimicrobial resistance (AMR) genes (e.g., *mecA*, *mecC*, *vanA*).
- Characterizing Mobile Genetic Elements (MGEs), including Staphylococcal Cassette Chromosome *mec* (SCC*mec*), bacteriophages, plasmids, and pathogenicity islands (SaPIs).
- Performing high-resolution molecular epidemiology and outbreak tracking.

### Genomic Benchmarks for *Staphylococcus aureus*
* **Genome Size:** ~2.75 to 2.95 Mbp (megabases)
* **Average GC Content:** ~32.7% to 33.0% (low-GC organism)
* **Expected Coding Sequences (CDS):** ~2,500 – 2,800 genes
* **rRNA Operons:** 5–6 operons (5S, 16S, 23S)
* **tRNAs:** 55–62 tRNA genes

## 3. Step-by-Step Methodology and Rationale

### Step 1: Read Quality Control and Preprocessing (`fastp`)
* **Tool:** `fastp` (v0.23+)
* **Command Script:** `scripts/run_qc.sh`
* **Key Parameters & Rationale:**
  * `--detect_adapter_for_pe`: Automatically detects adapter sequences from overlapping read pairs without requiring manual adapter sequence input.
  * `--qualified_quality_phred 20`: Enforces a base quality threshold of Q20 (99.0% base-calling accuracy). Bases below this score are tagged as unqualified.
  * `--unqualified_percent_limit 30`: Discards any read where more than 30% of bases fail the Q20 threshold.
  * `--trim_poly_g`: Specifically removes poly-G artifacts commonly generated by Illumina two-color chemistry (NextSeq / NovaSeq) when fluorescent signal decays at the 3' ends of reads.
  * `--correction`: Corrects mismatched base pairs in overlapping paired-end read regions where one read exhibits high confidence.
  * `--length_required 50`: Drops reads shorter than 50 bp post-trimming to prevent ambiguous k-mer mapping during assembly.

---

### Step 2: *De Novo* Assembly (`SPAdes`)
* **Tool:** `SPAdes` (v3.15+)
* **Command Script:** `scripts/run_assembly.sh`
* **Operational Mode:** `--isolate`
* **Rationale:**
  * Standard SPAdes settings are calibrated for multi-cell or single-cell datasets. The `--isolate` mode is engineered specifically for high-coverage, clonal bacterial isolates.
  * It employs an iterative multi-kmer de Bruijn graph approach (typically `k=21, 33, 55, 77, 99, 127`), capturing both fine-grained junctions and spanning repetitive sequence motifs.
  * `--isolate` includes an integrated mismatch correction step utilizing BWA to polish short insertions, deletions, and single-nucleotide errors before outputting final contigs.

---

### Step 3: Assembly Quality & Completeness Evaluation (`QUAST` & `BUSCO`)
* **Tools:**
  * `QUAST` (v5.2+) for continuity and topological metrics.
  * `BUSCO` (v5.4+) using the `staphylococcus_odb10` or `staphylococcus_odb12` lineage dataset for biological completeness.
* **Metric Interpretations:**
  * **N50:** The sequence length of the shortest contig at 50% of the total genome length. Higher N50 indicates greater continuity. For Illumina short-read *S. aureus* assemblies, an N50 > 50,000 bp is standard, while optimal assemblies exceed 150,000 bp.
  * **Total Length:** Must fall within 2.70 – 2.95 Mbp. Significant deviations indicate contamination (>3.1 Mbp) or severe under-assembly (<2.5 Mbp).
  * **Contig Count:** High-quality short-read assemblies typically yield 20–80 contigs (breaks usually occur at ribosomal RNA operon repeats).
  * **GC Content (%):** Should remain between 32.5% and 33.2%. Significant shifts (e.g., >35%) indicate non-staphylococcal DNA contamination.
  * **BUSCO Single-Copy Orthologs:** Evaluates core conserved staphylococcal genes. High-quality assemblies must score **Complete > 98.0%**, with fragmented and missing genes < 1.5%.

---

### Step 4: Rapid Prokaryotic Genome Annotation (`Prokka`)
* **Tool:** `Prokka` (v1.14+)
* **Command Script:** `scripts/run_annotation.sh`
* **Parameters & Database Hierarchy:**
  * `--genus Staphylococcus --species aureus`: Directs Prokka to load Staphylococcus-specific curated protein databases first.
  * `--compliant`: Forces GenBank / ENA / DDBJ compliant locus tag format and valid feature names.
  * **Feature Calling Modules:**
    1. `Prodigal`: Predicts protein coding sequences (CDS) using translation table 11 (Standard Bacterial).
    2. `Aragorn`: Identifies tRNA and tmRNA genes.
    3. `Barrnap`: Detects 5S, 16S, and 23S ribosomal RNA genes.
    4. Hierarchical BLAST/HMMER search against ISfinder, UniProt/Swiss-Prot, HAMAP, and Pfam.

---

### Step 5: Multi-Locus Sequence Typing (`mlst`)
* **Tool:** `mlst` (Torsten Seemann) utilizing PubMLST schemes
* **Command Script:** `scripts/run_annotation.sh`
* **Loci Tested (Enright et al. Scheme):**
  1. `arcC` (Carbamate kinase)
  2. `aroE` (Shikimate dehydrogenase)
  3. `glpF` (Glycerol kinase)
  4. `gmk` (Guanylate kinase)
  5. `pta` (Phosphate acetyltransferase)
  6. `tpi` (Triosephosphate isomerase)
  7. `yqiL` (Acetate kinase)
* **Epidemiological Value:** Determines the Sequence Type (ST) and Clonal Complex (CC), such as:
  * **ST8** (e.g. USA300, dominant North American CA-MRSA lineage)
  * **ST5** (e.g. N315 / Mu50, HA-MRSA lineage)
  * **ST250 / ST247** (e.g. COL, early historic MRSA isolates)
  * **ST239** (Global pandemic HA-MRSA clone)

## 4. Assembly Quality Control & Acceptance Matrix

| Parameter | Recommended Acceptance Threshold | Flag for Inspection | Likely Cause / Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Assembly Size** | 2.70 – 2.95 Mbp | < 2.60 Mbp or > 3.10 Mbp | Under-coverage or foreign species contamination |
| **GC Content (%)** | 32.5% – 33.2% | < 31.5% or > 34.0% | Metagenomic/taxonomic contamination (check with Kraken2) |
| **N50 Contig Size** | > 50,000 bp | < 25,000 bp | Fragmented assembly due to low coverage or sequencing bias |
| **Contig Count (< 500 bp excluded)** | < 100 contigs | > 150 contigs | Low library complexity or repetitive mobile element expansion |
| **BUSCO Completeness** | ≥ 98.0% | < 95.0% | Missing critical coding regions; re-sequence or re-assemble |
| **MLST Concordance** | 7/7 exact allele matches | Novel or missing alleles | Potential assembly break within an MLST housekeeping locus |
